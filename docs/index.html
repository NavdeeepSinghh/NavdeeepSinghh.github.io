Who I Am

I'm a final-year B.Tech Computer Engineering student at Thapar University, Patiala — working where machine learning, computer vision and real systems meet.

I spent the summer of 2025 at the Central Research Laboratory of Bharat Electronics Limited, shipping a containerized real-time computer vision service onto NVIDIA Jetson hardware. As Technical Head at the Institution of Engineers India, I run the tech events and workshops at Thapar. And I've presented research at ICIPAIMAR 2026.
